05/Major Mission Locations
Enemy Stronghold · Mission Location

Albion Drone Facility

Designed the full layout and mission structure for the Albion Drone Facility, one of the game’s key enemy strongholds. My work included crafting the facility’s architecture, sightlines, and infiltration paths to support multiple playstyles (stealth, combat, and drone hacking), while also integrating security systems, patrol routes, and environmental storytelling. I designed and scripted missions within the facility, balancing systemic gameplay with authored moments to ensure variety and challenge. The space was developed to function both as a central mission location and as a replayable open-world landmark, encouraging emergent encounters and player experimentation.

Clan Kelley HQ · Mission Location

The Sandstone Residence

Designed the layout and mission experience for The Sandstone Residence, a luxurious estate overtaken by Clan Kelley. I focused on creating an environment that blended opulence with menace, transforming the residence into a criminal stronghold while retaining the feel of a lived-in high-society home. My responsibilities included shaping the estate’s architecture and interior flow, designing infiltration routes with multiple entry points and vertical navigation opportunities, and integrating Kelley-specific enemy behaviors, security systems, and mission beats. The level was built to encourage a wide range of playstyles (stealth, combat, or hacking) while reinforcing the narrative theme of criminal corruption hidden beneath London’s surface.

Industrial Mission Space · Camden

Stafford Auto Garage

Designed the layout and mission flow for Stafford Auto Garage, an industrial automotive repair shop in Camden covertly used by Clan Kelley for their criminal operations. I crafted the space to feel authentic to a functioning garage while embedding layers of hostile occupation, including chop-shop machinery, makeshift security checkpoints, and Kelley contraband. My work involved shaping the garage’s floor plan to support multiple infiltration strategies, integrating environmental storytelling to reveal Kelley’s exploitation of the site, and building mission objectives such as sabotage, data collection, and vehicle recovery. The level emphasized vertical navigation through gantries and catwalks, creating tactical opportunities for stealth and hacking as well as intense close-quarters combat.

Corporate High-Rise · City of London

Inspire Towers

Developed the layout and mission design for Inspire Towers, a corporate high-rise in the City of London repurposed by Clan Kelley as a hub for their financial and trafficking operations. I structured the space to contrast sleek corporate offices with the gritty, illicit activities hidden within, using environmental storytelling to highlight how Kelley corrupted the building’s legitimate purpose. The level was designed to encourage multiple infiltration routes, with vertical traversal across office floors, rooftop access points, and hidden maintenance shafts providing different tactical approaches. Mission objectives included sabotaging Kelley’s operations, extracting critical data, and rescuing exploited captives, all while balancing stealth, hacking, and combat gameplay. The overall design emphasized the tension between modern London’s corporate face and the criminal underworld thriving within it.

07/Systems & Prototypes
Systemic London · World Design

Open World Systems & Set Dressing

Contributed to the systemic depth and authenticity of London by designing and placing a wide variety of world elements across the city. This included creating faction barricades for both Albion and Clan Kelley, building and positioning Albion propaganda stations, and staging protest events to reflect the political tension. I also implemented operative swap spawn points throughout the map, ensuring that when players switched characters, they experienced seamless and varied contextual animations that reinforced immersion and believability.
