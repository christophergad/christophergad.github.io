02/Overview

Asphalt 6: Adrenaline (Gameloft) – Contributed to multiple race tracks by designing layouts and gameplay elements such as speed boosts, ramps, and breakables to enhance flow and player engagement. Additionally, I worked on unlockable car skins to expand player customization, and created cinematic start and finish cameras to heighten the sense of spectacle and polish during races.
